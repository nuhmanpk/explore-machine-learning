How it works

Feel the river

Each boat has 5 feelers for rocks and logs, plus a sense of where the dock is and which way the water pushes.

A tiny brain

Those numbers go into a small brain that decides: how fast, and which way to steer.

Best boats win

Boats that reach the dock fast score most. Their brains are copied into the next round with small changes.

Change the river

Turn on a challenge. The old tricks stop working, so scores drop, until new babies find a way.

What just happened?

  • At first the boats drifted into rocks or got swept away. After a few rounds the best ones learned to aim upstream so the river carried them to the dock.
  • Each challenge broke the old plan, and the line on the chart fell. Then evolution found a new plan and the line climbed back up.
  • That is adapting: nobody re-programmed the boats. The world changed, and the learners changed with it.
For grown-ups: what's really going on

This is neuroevolution of a small feed-forward network [11 → 10 → 2] with tanh units.

  • Inputs: 5 ray-cast distances to rocks and logs (closer = bigger), the sine and cosine of the angle to the dock relative to the heading, distance to the dock, the water + wind velocity at the boat, and its own speed.
  • Outputs: throttle and rudder. Boat physics: speed eases toward the throttle; turning is stronger when moving; the current and wind are simply added to the position each step.
  • Fitness: 2 + (time left) for docking; otherwise closeness to the dock, scaled down after a crash.
  • Genetic algorithm: 60 genomes; the top 8% are copied unchanged (elitism); the rest are children of the top quarter (30% uniform crossover), with Gaussian mutation whose size is spread from small to large across the babies.
  • Adaptation: a challenge is a distribution shift. The population has no memory of the old river, but its diversity plus selection quickly re-tunes the policy, which is why scores drop and recover. Measured in Node (best half, 3 runs): stronger current 97–100% → 37–91% → 95–100%; wind 85–99% → 42–85% → 95–100%.
