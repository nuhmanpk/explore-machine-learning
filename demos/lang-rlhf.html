For grown-ups: what's really going on

This is RLHF (reinforcement learning from human feedback), the recipe used to align chat models like InstructGPT.

  • Preferences: each click is a pairwise comparison (A ≻ B).
  • Reward model: linear over features (8 trick one-hots + "fast"), trained with the Bradley–Terry loss −log σ(r(A) − r(B)). Accuracy is measured on 60 held-out pairs scored by the auto-human's hidden taste.
  • Policy: softmax over 16 actions (trick × speed). Updated with REINFORCE and a running-mean baseline: θ ← θ + η (r − b) ∇log π(a).
  • KL penalty: the slider pulls the logits back toward the original policy, approximating PPO's KL term. Without it, a flawed reward model gets exploited: reward hacking (try the buggy predictor).

Further reading: Ouyang et al., “Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback” (2022).
